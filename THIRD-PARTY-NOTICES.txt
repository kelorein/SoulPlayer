Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/first-person-arms-e3c42c05b22944e5839deb8e003f0987
License: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY; license version was not included in

recorder, cassette, or SoulPlayer animation data is part of the source asset.

FREE [FPS Arms] GameReady - RIGGED by BAMEN (Sketchfab user bamenwo05)
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/free-fps-arms-gameready-rigged-296d30fc705b4dff85c2c8a2d2724e7f
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Use: modified SoulPlayer first-person arms derivative. SoulPlayer preserves the articulated
arm/hand/finger rig, adds recorder/cassette sockets, rewires the supplied PBR textures, and
adds original SoulRecorder poses and animations. Its runtime renderer uses a derived
first-person mesh with the proximal shoulder-side sleeve geometry cropped and capped; the
complete source skeleton and the remaining vertex weights are retained. Changes were made
by the SoulPlayer project.

Only derived model/material data is packaged. The external master archives are not
redistributed with SoulPlayer. See Assets/SoulRecorder/source-manifest.json for immutable
source SHA-256 values and the transformation record.
